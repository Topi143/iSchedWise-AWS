# iSchedWise V4 Promo Video Implementation Guide

## Presentation Style Goal
Create a 75 to 90 second promo that feels like a clean PowerPoint presentation in motion: clear sections, headline-driven scenes, smooth transitions, and readable callouts for decision-makers.

## Audience
- School administrators
- Department deans
- Academic scheduling staff

## Visual Direction (PowerPoint-Inspired)
- Use slide-like chapter flow with a headline on each scene.
- Keep one message per scene.
- Use consistent lower-third style for feature labels.
- Use simple motion: fade, push, or zoom-in emphasis only.
- Keep spacing clean and text large enough for mobile preview.

## Audio and Edit Guidance
- Music: modern corporate pulse, light percussion, no aggressive drops.
- Voiceover: calm, clear, executive tone.
- SFX: very light UI click accents only.
- Highlights: use subtle focus boxes for critical actions.
- Recording: stable captures, zoom only on key controls.

## Production Checklist
- Capture real product screens only (no placeholder UI).
- Keep each feature clip under 10 seconds.
- Verify subtitle timing against final voiceover.
- Ensure all text is readable on mobile preview.
- Export one 1920x1080 landscape master.
- Export one 1080x1920 vertical cutdown.
- Deliver one full-length version and one 30-second cutdown.

## Dean-Only Subtitle Script (Markdown)

Use these as short on-screen subtitle lines for a dean-focused walkthrough.

### 01. Login
- Welcome to iSchedWise V4.
- Sign in with your username or email and password.
- Use Forgot Password if you need to recover access.

### 02. Forgot Password
- Enter your email address to request a reset link.
- Check your inbox and continue the secure password reset flow.
- Return to login and sign in with your updated password.

### 03. Dashboard
- This Dean Dashboard shows insights for your assigned programs.
- Track schedule completion, today’s classes, and quick priority actions.
- Use the program filter when you manage multiple programs.

### 04. Programs
- Review your program list and section totals.
- Open a program to manage sections by year level.
- Add, edit, or remove sections to keep class groupings updated.

### 05. Curriculum
- Review curriculum structure by year level and semester.
- Maintain subjects in existing curricula: add, edit, or delete entries.
- Curriculum creation and bulk import are administrator workflows.

### 06. Faculty
- Manage faculty profiles, teaching load, and availability windows.
- Assign or unassign subjects based on department scope.
- Export faculty line-up files for coordination and reporting.

### 07. Buildings
- Manage buildings and their rooms from one workspace.
- Add, edit, or remove rooms and update room metadata.
- Keep room status accurate for scheduling and conflict checks.

### 08. Schedules - Create New
- Build class or exam schedules from a unified form.
- Assign section, subject, faculty, room, day, and time.
- Use batch generation tools to speed up schedule drafting.

### 09. Schedules - Class View
- Review class schedules in table and calendar views.
- Filter by program, year level, and section for focused checking.
- Edit, export, snapshot, and clear schedules within your allowed scope.

### 10. Schedules - Faculty View
- Inspect each faculty timetable and workload distribution.
- Check overlaps and availability before finalizing assignments.
- Export views for meetings and faculty coordination.

### 11. Schedules - Room View
- Monitor room occupancy across buildings and time windows.
- Spot room conflicts early and adjust assignments quickly.
- Export room-based schedule views for operations planning.

### 12. Schedules - Exam View
- Organize exam schedules by date, period, room, and section.
- Validate exam allocations and avoid timing conflicts.
- Export and snapshot exam schedules for controlled releases.

### 13. Reports - Overview
- See high-level scheduling metrics and completion trends.
- Apply program filters to focus on your academic scope.
- Use visual summaries to support planning decisions.

### 14. Reports - Faculty
- Analyze faculty workload, utilization, and availability status.
- Identify overload and underload before final schedule approval.
- Export report outputs for department planning.

### 15. Reports - Rooms
- Analyze room utilization, peak usage, and underused spaces.
- Compare allocation patterns to improve room planning.
- Export room utilization outputs for facilities coordination.

### 16. Reports - Weekly
- Review weekly class and exam distribution patterns.
- Detect crowded days and rebalance schedule load.
- Use weekly summaries to guide operational planning.

### 17. Reports - Compare
- Compare academic periods side by side.
- Evaluate changes in schedules, exams, faculty load, and room usage.
- Use trend visuals to support semester strategy discussions.

### 18. Archives - Overview
- View archive totals without cluttering active workspaces.
- Check archived categories across schedules, curriculum, faculty, and facilities.
- Restore and permanent delete actions are administrator-controlled.

### 19. Archives - Schedules
- Search archived class and exam records with filters.
- Review archive metadata, including who archived and when.
- Coordinate with administrators for restore or delete actions.

### 20. Archives - Curriculum
- Browse archived curriculum records by keyword and context.
- Verify historical curriculum details when auditing changes.
- Coordinate with administrators for restore or delete actions.

### 21. Archives - Programs
- Browse archived program records and history details.
- Confirm archived program context before future planning.
- Coordinate with administrators for restore or delete actions.

### 22. Archives - Faculty
- Review archived faculty records and associated metadata.
- Use archive history for staffing continuity checks.
- Coordinate with administrators for restore or delete actions.

### 23. Archives - Buildings
- Review archived buildings and room-related history.
- Keep reference access to retired facilities data.
- Coordinate with administrators for restore or delete actions.

### 24. Account
- Update your profile and contact information.
- Change password with current-password verification.
- Enable or disable 2FA and manage trusted devices.

### 25. Settings
- View active academic settings in dean read-only mode.
- Update department details where dean access is allowed.
- Save display preferences such as text size and dark mode.
- System-wide configuration remains administrator-controlled.

### 26. Access Boundary Note
- Dean access is scoped to assigned programs.
- User management and activity logs are administrator-only.
- Archive restore/delete and full system settings remain admin-controlled.
