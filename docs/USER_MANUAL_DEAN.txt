==============================================================================
                    iSchedWise V4 - DEAN USER MANUAL
==============================================================================

Note: As a Dean, your view is filtered to show only the
programs and departments assigned to you by the Admin.


1. LOGIN PAGE
Screenshot needed: Login Page

Enter your username or email and password to sign in.
Check "Remember me" to stay signed in across sessions.
Click the eye icon to show or hide your password.
If you forgot your password, click "Forgot Password?" to
receive a reset link via email.


2. FORGOT PASSWORD
Screenshot needed: Forgot Password Page

Enter your registered email address and click "Send Reset
Link." You will receive an email with a link to reset your
password. Follow the link and create a new password.


3. FIRST LOGIN SETUP
Screenshot needed: First Login Setup Page

If your account was created by an admin and you are logging
in for the first time, you will be asked to set up your
account. Enter a valid email address and create a secure
password (8-30 characters). Click "Complete Setup" to
activate your account.


4. DASHBOARD
Screenshot needed: Dashboard Page (Dean view)

The dashboard shows an overview of the system filtered to
your assigned programs. You can see stat cards for class
schedules, exam schedules, faculty workload, and room
utilization with trend indicators compared to the previous
semester. Quick action buttons appear based on current
system status. Scroll down to view the weekly activity chart
and recent activity log.


5. PROGRAMS
Screenshot needed: Programs Page (Dean view, showing only assigned programs)

The Programs page shows only the programs assigned to you.
The left panel lists your programs. Click a program to view
its details on the right panel.

View Program Details - See the program code, name,
department, year levels, and sections.

Manage Sections - View, add, edit, or remove sections within
your assigned programs. Each section has a name and student
count.

Add Program - You can add new programs within your assigned
department.

Edit Program - Modify program details such as program code,
name, and year levels.

Toggle Status - Activate or deactivate a program.

Archive Program - Archive a program with a reason. This will
also archive its curricula and soft-delete its schedules.


6. CURRICULUM
Screenshot needed: Curriculum Page (Dean view)

The Curriculum page shows curricula for your assigned
programs only.

View Curriculum - Expand a curriculum to see its year
levels, semesters, and subjects.

Manage Subjects - Add, edit, or delete subjects within
semesters of your assigned curricula. Each subject has a
code, name, units, lecture hours, and lab hours.

Add Curriculum - Create a new curriculum for your assigned
programs.

Edit Curriculum - Modify curriculum details such as code,
year levels, and semesters.

Archive Curriculum - Archive a curriculum with a reason. It
can be restored from the Archives page.


7. FACULTIES
Screenshot needed: Faculty Page (Dean view, filtered by department)

The Faculty page shows only faculty in your assigned
departments. The left panel lists faculty members filtered
by your department.

View Faculty Details - Click a faculty member to see their
information. The detail panel has three tabs:
  - Info Tab: Personal information and department
  - Assignments Tab: Current semester subject assignments
    with units per subject
  - Schedule Tab: All schedules showing days, times, rooms,
    and sections

View Workload - The workload summary shows current load,
maximum allowed units, utilization percentage, and a
color-coded status (green for normal, yellow for warning,
red for exceeded).

Add Faculty - Add a new faculty member to your department.

Edit Faculty - Modify personal info or department assignment.

Archive Faculty - Archive with a reason. The faculty member
can be restored from Archives.


8. BUILDINGS
Screenshot needed: Buildings Page

The Buildings page shows all buildings and rooms available
in the system.

View Buildings - See the list of buildings and their rooms
with room numbers and types.

Add Building - Create a new building.

Add Room - Add a room to a building with room number and
type (Lecture, Lab, etc.).

Edit - Modify building names or room details.

Archive Building - Archive a building with a reason. This
also archives all rooms and soft-deletes related schedules.


9. SCHEDULES - CREATE NEW
Screenshot needed: Schedule Create/Form Page

Create new class or exam schedules for your assigned
programs.

Create Class Schedule - Select the section (from your
assigned programs), subject, faculty member (shows workload
percentage), room, day of the week, start time, end time,
and schedule type. The system checks for conflicts:
  - Section time conflicts
  - Faculty double-booking
  - Room double-booking

Create Exam Schedule - Select the section, subject, faculty
proctor, room, exam date (within the exam period), start
time, and end time. The system validates date, time, and
conflict constraints.


10. SCHEDULES - CLASS VIEW
Screenshot needed: Schedule Class View Page

View class schedules organized by section. Select a program
from the filter dropdown (shows only your assigned
programs), then select a section.

The schedule table shows subject code, faculty, room, day,
start time, end time, and type.

Edit Schedule - Click edit on any row to modify the schedule.

Delete Schedule - Soft-delete a schedule.

Export - Download schedules as Excel or PDF.


11. SCHEDULES - FACULTY VIEW
Screenshot needed: Schedule Faculty View Page

View all courses assigned to a faculty member in the current
semester. Select a department (your assigned departments)
and then a faculty member.

The detail panel shows the workload summary and a table of
all schedules with subject code, section, day, time, and
room.

Export - Download as Excel or PDF.


12. SCHEDULES - ROOM VIEW
Screenshot needed: Schedule Room View Page

View all classes scheduled in a room. Select a building and
then a room.

The detail panel shows room info, utilization summary, and
a schedule table with all classes in that room.

Export - Download as Excel or PDF.


13. SCHEDULES - EXAM VIEW
Screenshot needed: Schedule Exam View Page

View exam schedules for the current exam period. Select a
program (your assigned programs) and section.

The table shows subject, section, date, start time, end
time, room, and proctor.

Add, edit, or delete exam schedules from this view.

Export - Download as Excel or PDF.


14. REPORTS - OVERVIEW
Screenshot needed: Reports Overview Page (Dean view)

The Reports Overview shows a summary dashboard filtered to
your assigned programs. Stat cards display class schedules,
exam schedules, faculty count, and room utilization. The
Schedule Quality Index (SQI) provides a composite score.
AI-powered insights offer recommendations based on your
department data.


15. REPORTS - FACULTY WORKLOAD
Screenshot needed: Reports Faculty Workload Page

View faculty workload for your assigned departments. Each
faculty member is listed with units assigned, maximum
allowed, utilization percentage, and status badge. A
workload distribution chart and department breakdown are
included.

Export - Download as Excel or PDF.


16. REPORTS - ROOMS
Screenshot needed: Reports Rooms Page

View room utilization metrics including hours occupied per
day, percentage of available time, and class counts. A room
heatmap shows busy times. Filter by building or room type.

Export - Download as Excel or PDF.


17. REPORTS - WEEKLY
Screenshot needed: Reports Weekly Distribution Page

View the distribution of classes across days of the week
for your assigned programs. See classes per day, peak days,
and balance analysis.

Export - Download as Excel or PDF.


18. REPORTS - COMPARE
Screenshot needed: Reports Compare Page

Compare schedule data between different semesters or
academic years for your assigned programs. View side-by-side
comparisons of key metrics.


19. ARCHIVES - OVERVIEW
Screenshot needed: Archives Overview Page

View a dashboard with statistics on all archived items
including charts by type and recent archive actions.


20. ARCHIVES - SCHEDULES
Screenshot needed: Archives Schedules Page

View all archived class and exam schedules. Filter by
academic year, semester, schedule type, section, faculty,
room, or program.

Restore - Click Restore to unarchive a schedule.

Permanently Delete - Click Delete to permanently remove.

Export - Download filtered results as Excel or PDF.


21. ARCHIVES - CURRICULUM
Screenshot needed: Archives Curriculum Page

View archived curricula with program, code, date, and
reason.

Restore - Unarchive a curriculum.

Permanently Delete - Remove permanently.


22. ARCHIVES - PROGRAMS
Screenshot needed: Archives Programs Page

View archived programs with code, name, department, date,
and reason.

Restore - Unarchive a program.

Permanently Delete - Remove permanently.


23. ARCHIVES - FACULTY
Screenshot needed: Archives Faculty Page

View archived faculty with name, department, date, and
reason.

Restore - Reactivate a faculty member.

Permanently Delete - Remove permanently.


24. ARCHIVES - BUILDINGS
Screenshot needed: Archives Buildings Page

View archived buildings with name, room count, date, and
reason.

Restore - Unarchive a building and its rooms.

Permanently Delete - Remove permanently.


25. ACCOUNT (PROFILE)
Screenshot needed: Account/Profile Page

Edit Profile - Update your full name, email address, or
username. Click Save to apply changes.

Change Password - Enter your current password, then enter
and confirm a new password (8-30 characters). Click "Change
Password" to update.


26. SETTINGS
Screenshot needed: Settings Page

View and configure system-wide parameters.

Academic Settings - View or change the active academic year
and semester.

Schedule Time Settings - View the schedule start/end hours,
lunch break times, and operation days.

Exam Settings - View or configure exam period dates, hours,
and duration limits.

Faculty Load Settings - View the default faculty load limit
and warning threshold.


27. SIDEBAR NAVIGATION
Screenshot needed: Sidebar (Dean view, showing available menu items)

The sidebar is always visible on the left side. As a Dean,
you can access:

  Dashboard
  Programs
  Curriculum
  Faculties
  Buildings
  Schedules (submenu: Create New, Class, Faculty, Room, Exam)
  Reports (submenu: Overview, Faculty, Rooms, Weekly,
           Compare)
  Archives (submenu: Overview, Schedules, Curriculum,
            Programs, Faculty, Buildings)
  Account
  Settings

Note: The "Users" page and "Activity" report are not
available to Dean accounts.

At the bottom, toggle dark mode and adjust text size using
the slider. Your preferences are saved automatically.
